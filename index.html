Core Calculation Methodologies & Formulas Explained

Every tool on Zeta Calculator is built upon peer-reviewed financial mathematics, international health standards, and deterministic scientific equations. We eliminate guesswork and black-box estimation by providing fully transparent formula implementations:

Loan Amortization & Reducing Balance EMI

Our loan calculators utilize the standard banking reducing-balance amortization model. Monthly interest is calculated strictly on the remaining outstanding principal:
Interest_m = Principal_remaining × (Annual_Rate / 12). Each payment proportionally reduces the principal balance, accelerating equity build-up over the lifespan of the loan.

Compound Interest & Future Value (FV)

For long-term wealth planning and systematic investment plans (SIP), we apply compound interest formulas adjusted for monthly compounding frequencies:
FV = P × (1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)]. This models exponential wealth accumulation, dividend reinvestment, and real purchasing power after inflation.

Progressive Income Tax & Effective Rate

Tax liabilities are calculated using tiered marginal tax brackets rather than flat percentages. Gross income is adjusted by standard deductions and exemptions to determine taxable income. The engine breaks down the effective tax rate versus marginal tax rate to help with year-end tax planning.

Metabolic Health: BMR, TDEE, and BMI

Health calculators implement the clinically validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation for Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and standard World Health Organization (WHO) classifications for Body Mass Index (BMI). Activity multipliers accurately compute Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) for weight management.

Construction, Surface Area & Materials

Square footage, concrete volume, tile counts, and paint coverage estimations incorporate industry-standard waste factors (typically 5% to 15%). Users can input dimensions in feet, inches, meters, or centimeters with instantaneous unit conversion.

Everyday Travel & Fuel Economy

Trip calculators account for distance, vehicle efficiency (MPG, km/L, or L/100km), fuel price per gallon or liter, and passenger count to distribute road trip transportation costs fairly among travelers.

How is monthly loan EMI calculated?

Monthly EMI is calculated using the standard mathematical formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is the Principal Loan Amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the tenure in months.
